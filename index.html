About Reno Cricket Club

MORE THAN A SPORT.

Reno Cricket Club is the heartbeat of cricket in the Reno area and Greater Northern Nevada. We welcome players of different ages, backgrounds and skill levels while building a culture centered on sportsmanship, community involvement and the growth of cricket.

Our community reflects roots from India, the United States, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan and beyond. RCC works to make cricket a unifying force in Northern Nevada through organized competition, volunteering, community engagement and responsible club governance.
